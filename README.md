Talaivosa Moala
Talaivosa Moala is a healthcare administrator and community-focused professional based in Honolulu, Hawaii, with a background that spans caregiving, public health, and student services. She currently owns and operates Cereus Adult Residential Care Home, where she manages daily operations, ensures compliance with Hawaii State Department of Health regulations, coordinates care with medical providers, and oversees the home's finances and administrative needs. Prior to this, she operated Cereus Foster Care Home for four years, providing direct care to elderly residents while managing billing, compliance, and coordination with healthcare providers.

Alongside her work in residential care, Talaivosa has served as a Student Services Specialist at the University of Hawaii at Manoa's Counseling and Student Development Center since 2016, where she supports front-line triage for students facing urgent mental health and safety concerns, manages data and reporting systems, and assists in the day-to-day operations of the Center. Her time at UH Manoa also includes prior roles as a Receptionist/Clerk Typist and Proctor for national examinations, reflecting a long-standing connection to the university community.

Talaivosa holds a Master of Public Health, a Bachelor of Arts in Biology, and a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science, all from the University of Hawaii at Manoa, and is currently pursuing an Executive MBA with an anticipated graduation of May 2028. She is a Certified Nurse Aide and a graduate of Roosevelt High School. Beyond her professional work, she is active in her community and church, currently serving as District Secretary for the United Women of Faith and Assistant to the Treasurer at First United Methodist Church, where she also contributes to the Multimedia Team. Her volunteer history includes years of service at the church's foodbank, coordination of campus events at UH Manoa, and involvement in interview committees for the university's College of Opportunities Program.
