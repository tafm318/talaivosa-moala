# Research Brief: Financial Sustainability of a Family-Run Care Home

## Research Problem
My biggest financial challenge in operating my care home is maintaining consistent resident occupancy. My current care home has space for two residents. When the care home goes from having two residents to one, the income decreases but many of the expenses remain the same.

## Why This Problem Matters
This problem matters because this business is my family's livelihood. It's the only type of business that my family has the skills for. If the care home business were to close, our family's housing situation would become less stable.

## Economic Concepts
### Fixed cost
Definition: an expense that does not change with the level of business activity.
Example: My rent is an example of a fixed cost because whether my care home has one resident or two residents, the cost of my rent does not change.


### Variable Cost
Definition: an expense that changes as the level of business activity changes.
Example: My grocery expense is an example of a variable cost because if my care home has two residents, it costs more than having just one resident.


### Marginal Analysis
Definition: comparing the additional benefits and additional costs of making a decision.
Example: Having a second resident means additional income and additional costs. Additional costs include grocery expenses, time, and labor. Accepting a second resident makes financial sense if the additional income that the second resident brings in is greater than the additional costs.


### Opportunity Cost
Definition: the value of the next-best alternative you give up when you make a decision. 
Example: Working at a care home limits my opportunities to socialize and interact with others.
