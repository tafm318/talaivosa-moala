# Perfect Competition — Stage 3 Analysis

## Stage 2 Results
My Stage 2 model identified an optimal planting mix of 10 tomato beds, 20 carrot beds, and 30 mesclun beds. The calculated seasonal profit was $42,775.16.

My original hypothesis was 16 tomato beds, 20 carrot beds, and 28 mesclun beds. The model recommended fewer tomato beds because the additional labor and fertilizer costs reduced their profitability.

## Economic Analysis
The model shows that planting more than 10 tomato beds becomes less profitable because labor requirements continue to increase. Even though tomatoes generate $8,800 in revenue per bed, the additional labor and fertilizer costs reduce the benefit of planting more tomatoes. This demonstrates the importance of comparing marginal revenue with marginal cost when making production decisions.## Figures
